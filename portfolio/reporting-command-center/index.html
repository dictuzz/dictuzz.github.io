Reporting Command Center

A fictional internal platform concept for consolidating fragmented weekly reporting into one operating rhythm leaders could actually trust.

Role Systems architect, reporting lead, workflow designer
Scope Weekly reporting, exception handling, executive summaries
Tools Excel, Google Sheets, Apps Script, dashboard logic
Impact 62% faster prep time, clearer ownership, fewer reporting misses

The brief

Leadership needed a single, dependable reporting layer. Teams were sending updates in different formats, on different schedules, with different levels of detail. The result was friction at the exact moment decisions needed speed.

The challenge

The problem was not only technical. It was behavioral. Each function had built its own way of reporting, which meant any redesign had to feel lighter, not more controlling.

The approach

I designed a three-layer structure: operational inputs for teams, review checkpoints for managers, and an executive summary view for leaders. That separation made the system easier to adopt because each layer served a specific decision horizon.

What changed

Weekly preparation time dropped sharply, missing fields became visible earlier, and the reporting ritual itself became calmer. The best outcome was cultural: teams started trusting the process because it reduced noise instead of adding ceremony.

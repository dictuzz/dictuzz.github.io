Community Outreach System

A fictional engagement system for turning scattered outreach efforts into a more coherent member journey with stronger participation and follow-through.

Role Journey designer, communications lead, operations partner
Scope Onboarding, reminders, event cadence, retention touchpoints
Audience New members, recurring participants, volunteer leads
Impact 28% lift in repeat attendance across one cycle

The brief

Outreach was active, but not coherent. New members received inconsistent information, reminders arrived too late, and the team had no shared view of where people were dropping off after their first touchpoint.

The challenge

Community systems break when they feel transactional. The architecture had to support consistency while still preserving warmth, belonging, and personal attention.

The approach

I framed the system around key moments: first contact, first event, first follow-up, and continued participation. Each stage received clearer owner logic, better messaging timing, and more deliberate handoffs between core team members.

What changed

The experience became more legible for both members and organizers. Follow-up quality improved, reminders felt more intentional, and the team could finally see where retention depended on system design rather than last-minute effort.

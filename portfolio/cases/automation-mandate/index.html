Workflow Automation · Home Digital Indonesia

The Automation Mandate

Took a person-dependent reporting cycle and turned it into something the team could run the same way every Monday — without thinking about it.

By  Benedictus Richard Ricardo Management Trainee · Home Digital Indonesia Jul 2024 – Sep 2025 · Jakarta, Indonesia

Three departments were losing time to the same reporting routine every week. The fix was less about software and more about giving the process real rules.

Context

Finance, sales, and ops were all touching the same data, reformatting the same spreadsheets, handing off the same half-finished files. It worked, technically — but it ate too much time and broke whenever someone forgot a step.

What was built

I treated the workflow like a system, not just a spreadsheet. Rebuilt the workbook logic in Excel, wrote SOPs so the same steps happen every cycle, and mapped the whole reporting path end-to-end. The point was that anyone on the team could run it the same way every Monday without thinking.

Outcome

About twelve hours of manual work came off the weekly rhythm. But the bigger win was that people stopped dreading Mondays. The cadence was cleaner, handoffs stopped breaking, and the process could survive someone being out sick.

Twelve hours of manual work, gone. Replaced with a routine anyone on the team can run.

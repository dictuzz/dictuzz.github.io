Context

Finance, sales, and operations were collecting the same information, reformatting spreadsheets, and handing off incomplete files. The process worked, but it required significant time and depended too heavily on individual memory.

What was built

I redesigned the Excel workbook logic and documented shared procedures for each step. I also mapped the reporting workflow end to end so the team could follow the same process each Monday with fewer errors and handoff delays.

Outcome

The redesigned workflow removed about 12 hours of manual work each week. It also clarified responsibilities and allowed the team to maintain the process when someone was away.

Training & Readiness · Vertical Rescue Indonesia

The quieter part of high-risk work is the part nobody films.

Four-plus years of equipment care and training support at Vertical Rescue Indonesia — readiness as a discipline, not a posture.

By  Benedictus Richard Ricardo Assistant Instructor · Vertical Rescue Indonesia Jan 2021 – Present · Indonesia

Readiness work doesn't get filmed. But when things go wrong in the field, it's the only thing that matters.

Context

In technical rescue, you can't improvise once things get serious. Equipment has to be checked, training has to be consistent, and everyone needs to know their role before anyone is actually in danger.

What was built

I took care of equipment, supported climbing sessions as lead climber and belayer, and helped keep the training standard high. Not glamorous work — just the kind that has to be right every time.

Outcome

Equipment stayed ready. Training sessions ran more smoothly. And the people coming into the program had a better foundation before they ever hit a real situation.

In technical environments, confidence comes from preparation — not improvisation.

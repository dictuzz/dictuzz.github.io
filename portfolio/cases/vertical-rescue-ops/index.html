Training & Readiness · Vertical Rescue Indonesia

Readiness is built long before a field assignment.

More than four years supporting equipment care and technical training at Vertical Rescue Indonesia. Readiness depends on consistent checks and disciplined preparation.

By  Benedictus Richard Ricardo Assistant Instructor · Vertical Rescue Indonesia Jan 2021 – Present · Indonesia

Readiness work happens out of view, but it determines whether equipment, people, and procedures are dependable when conditions become difficult.

Context

Technical rescue depends on disciplined preparation. Equipment checks, regular practice, and clear roles need to be in place before conditions become challenging.

What was built

I maintain and inspect equipment, support technical climbing sessions as a lead climber and belayer, and help new trainees develop safe, repeatable practices.

Outcome

Consistent equipment checks and training support strengthen operational readiness and give new trainees a more reliable foundation before field exercises.

In technical work, confidence comes from preparation and consistency.

Evidence

Context

When a stage transition is unclear, the audience notices immediately. The challenge at a large public event is to keep timing, technical cues, and handoffs reliable under pressure.

What was built

I translated the running order into clear steps for the technical crew, speakers, and performers. This included planning transitions in advance and coordinating teams throughout the event.

Outcome

The work improved transition clarity and helped the stage team maintain the schedule under pressure. At the 2025 youth service, I hosted more than 500 attendees while coordinating the live program.

Consistent timing makes a live event feel considered. Unclear transitions are immediately visible.

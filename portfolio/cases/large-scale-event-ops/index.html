Large-Scale Event Ops

Kept live execution stable across 1,000+ attendee environments and high-pressure transitions; MC-led Youth Mass 2025 for 500+ participants while maintaining pacing and clarity on stage.

By Benedictus Richard Ricardo Stage Coordinator & MC · Ultimate Event Organizer 2022 – 2025 · Jakarta, ID

Large public events compress time, pressure, and risk into a single live window. My work focused on keeping execution stable when the room could not afford confusion.

Live programs with large audiences depend on precise transitions, shared timing, and clean backstage coordination. Small breakdowns become visible immediately when the environment is live. There is no quiet draft. A transition that is even slightly unclear becomes a visible failure the moment the room is watching.

How I operated

My role sat close to the line between planning and execution. I helped translate the show flow into something technical teams and on-stage participants could actually run without hesitation — coordinating stage and backstage execution, supporting transition planning, and helping maintain the pacing needed for technical teams, speakers, performers, and audiences to stay in sync.

When pacing holds, the event feels intentional. When it breaks, everyone notices.

Why it mattered

The work mattered because consistency is what protects the audience experience. Events ran with less transition downtime, clearer stage flow, and more dependable control during moments where timing and execution had to hold under pressure — including Youth Mass 2025, which served as a clear proof point for live communication in front of 500+ participants.
